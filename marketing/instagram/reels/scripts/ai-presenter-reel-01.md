## 4. Editing (CapCut / InShot)
1. Order: S1 → S2 → B-roll A → S3 → B-roll B → S4 → B-roll C → S5 → S6. Keep about 2–3 seconds of each B-roll clip and place it under the presenter's voice.
2. Captions: use auto captions with 2–4 words at a time, bold uppercase, white text with key words in **orange #FE6519** (the reference reel uses yellow; use your brand orange).
3. Pop-up text on the right beats: "20+ COURIERS", "₹28 SE", "WHATSAPP COD CONFIRM", "NDR RE-ATTEMPT", "DEDICATED MANAGER".
4. Add a whoosh sound on every cut and a punch-in zoom on the bold words, with a trending beat at low volume under the voice.
5. End card (2 seconds): the Vishwaship logo + "vishwaship.in" + "COD • Parcels ₹28 se • Cargo ₹8/kg se". Small print: "*Final rates depend on route, weight, size, service and taxes."
6. When posting, turn on Instagram's **AI info / "Made with AI"** label, because the presenter is AI-generated.
