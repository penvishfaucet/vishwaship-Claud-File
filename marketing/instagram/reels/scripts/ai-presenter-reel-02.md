## D. Editing style (CapCut)
- **Captions:** two layers, like the reference reel.
  - Bottom: auto captions, 2–3 words at a time, bold condensed font, uppercase, white with key words in orange #FE6519, and a thick black outline.
  - Middle: a short serif phrase (e.g. "Best price par", "Deliver kar paao") that fades in on key lines.
- **Pace:** a cut or zoom every 1.5–2 seconds, no pauses between lines, and a slight speed-up (1.1×) if the AI voice is slow.
- **SFX:** a whoosh on every cut, a bass hit on the hook, "ding" on the logo, "cha-ching" on 1.5%, and a pop on emoji stickers.
- **Music:** a trending energetic beat at about 15% volume under the voice.
- **Brand:** the Vishwaship logo watermark in the top-left corner (small) through the whole reel.
- **Posting:** turn on Instagram's **AI info** label, because the presenter is AI-generated.
